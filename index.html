MSc Thesis
2025

Generative Models for Probabilistic Energy Forecasting ↗

AIMS Rwanda · Supervised by Prof. Michael Melgaard (University of Sussex) · Distinction

Renewable energy output swings with the weather, and a single-number forecast hides that risk. I built a Conditional Wasserstein GAN that generates full forecast distributions from weather inputs, and benchmarked it against quantile regression, expectile regression and mixture density networks. Evaluated on pinball loss, calibration and interval coverage, across time-based and random splits.

Applied Research
2026

Understanding Customer Voice in Low-Resource Languages

Equity Bank Rwanda · Kinyarwanda, French & English

The gap. Most NLP models and translation systems are built for high-resource languages. Real customer feedback in Rwanda mixes Kinyarwanda, French and English, often in the same sentence, with informal spelling. Off-the-shelf tools handle this poorly, and when models are checked through machine translation, translation errors can quietly distort the labels.

What I did. Fine-tuned AfroXLM-R with attention-based output heads that predict sentiment, aspect and intent jointly, reaching an F1 score of 0.83. Built a translation layer into English for human validation, and deployed the model as an interactive demo.

Open questions I want to pursue

  • How much does machine-translation error distort sentiment and intent labels in low-resource languages?
  • Can multi-task learning across related labels make up for scarce annotated data?
  • Can the model quantify its own uncertainty on code-switched text, so low-confidence predictions go to a human?
  • Low-resource NLP
  • Code-switching
  • Multi-task learning
  • Transformers

2025

Mathematical Foundations of DeepSeek-R1 ↗

MSc presentation · AIMS Rwanda

How DeepSeek reaches strong reasoning at low cost. The core is Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO): it builds on PPO's clipped objective but normalises each sampled answer's reward against its group, removing the critic network and reducing gradient variance. Also covers Mixture-of-Experts layers, multi-token prediction and speculative decoding.
